L1 (per SM).   Caches global loads in 128 - B lines with 32 - B sectors. It is not 
kept coherent across SMs, and it does not hold dirty global data: stores are 
forwarded to L2. 
L2 (per GPU).   All DRAM traffic passes through it. Write - back: modified 
sectors go to DRAM when evicted. Per - byte masks track partially written 
sectors. 
Stores coalesce like loads.   32 consecutive 4 - B stores from a warp → 4 full 
sectors. Scattered partial - sector writes can cost extra DRAM work (e.g. read - 
modify - write with ECC). 
Caches are not a substitute for coalescing.   They help when data is reused 
soon, but they cannot recover the bandwidth wasted by fetching mostly 
unused sectors.
